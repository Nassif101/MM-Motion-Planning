**Where twins first part** (campaign A; chicane: F2 `plan_seed` 20, B5 `end` 20). The histogram of the first
diverging decision: F2 `plan_seed` 130 of 140, `plan_homotopy` 3,
none 7; B5 `status` / `gate_unsafe` 60 (gate trips at a pair outside its margin asking just over 5 mm, e.g. 4.94
against 5.05 mm), `end` 26, none 54. Both histograms mislead:
- **F2's `plan_seed` divergences are mostly ties.** At the first one the two best feasible merits of one side differ by
  a median 1.2e-12 relative (about 80 % below 2e-11; 20 of 130 above 1e-10, the largest 0.048). Most are likely one
  solution listed twice: the planner merges seeds whose control points agree within 0.02, but the log does not carry
  the `merged` flag, so the table cannot tell. The label of the winner is then arbitrary.
- **Most flips are continuous, not a discrete choice.** In 12 of the 15 flipped pairs the plans' paths first part by
  more than 1 mm when the two robots are 0.1-1.3 mm apart (door_then_beam s15: 0.02 mm), and in 11 of the 13 that are
  not feasibility flips the same seed wins on both sides there (store_room s14, s16 differ). Tie labels often differ
  earlier; the first non-tie label difference comes at or after the path's parting. The medium planner amplifies
  sub-millimetre state differences continuously.
- **Two flips are feasibility flips:** gate_right_swing s7 (12.4 s): `fold_back` is infeasible in one twin (merit
  11.29) and feasible in the other (1.82, against `previous` 0.69), and that twin takes the costlier `fold_back`
  (presumably the waiting rule); door_then_beam s18 (15.6 s): both seeds infeasible in one twin, `fold_back` feasible
  in the other. The robot states differ by at most one float32 step (9.5e-7 m) up to there; plans then 0.21 and 0.33 m
  apart, one twin reaches, the other holds. A threshold crossed by a measured value (spec 3.4: enter/leave thresholds
  or a dwell), not a merit band.
- **B5's gate trips never change an outcome** (0 flips): B5 fails store_room and both low beams in every run, plain or
  twin, and passes the rest.

**Refined (CP1, user decision; corrected after the refinement's review).** `divergence.py` now treats two winners as
one solution when their plans agree within 1 mm, or when their merits agree within 1e-9 relative or one is merged; it
adds `plan_path` (the plan's path parting by more than 1 mm) and notes where the sets of feasible seeds first differ
(not a decision: 49 of the 63 pairs whose first difference was there kept the winner and changed nothing). Re-run on
the same logs, first diverging decision, campaign A: F2 `plan_path` 104, `plan_seed` 20, `plan_homotopy` 2,
`plan_status` 1, none 13; B5 unchanged. The 15 flips:

| Kind | Flips | First divergence | Robots apart there |
|---|---|---|---|
| discrete | 2 | gate_right_swing s7: `plan_seed` `previous` against `fold_back` (feasible in one twin only, merit 1.82 against 11.29; `fold_back` wins on progress); door_then_beam s18: `plan_status` infeasible against ok | below 1e-6 m |
| continuous | 13 | `plan_path` 11 (plans 3.5-43 mm apart under the same winner), `plan_seed` 2 (store_room s14, s16, distinct plans) | 0.02-1.5 mm |

Commitment can target the two discrete flips (a seed's or the plan's feasibility at its threshold); the thirteen
continuous ones are the medium planner amplifying sub-millimetre state differences, outside what commitment fixes.
(The first version of this table counted four discrete flips: store_room s5 and s13 were the same plan under two labels,
11 and 0.24 um apart.)

## Gate rule

`slack_gate_inside: absolute` ([configs/b5_absolute.yaml](configs/b5_absolute.yaml)), `e1_ensembles.sh variant ... abs`,
`experiment_runs/e1/abs`, 2026-11-08 20:47-22:34 (1340 runs, none failed), against the frozen set at the same
settings. Reports in `experiment_runs/e1/reports`.

**A1, holds through the last 10 s** (`compare_variants.py locks`; `lock` = the step's lock: the gate fails at a pair
less than 5 mm inside its margin on plans asking at most 5 mm, which the absolute rule passes; `bound` = such a pair
with plans asking more than 5 mm, a hold at the absolute rule's depth bound):

| Set | Frozen: lock / bound / deep / outside | Absolute: lock / bound / deep / outside |
|---|---|---|
| campaign A (plain runs) | 8 / 8 / 21 / 37 | **0** / 10 / 27 / 36 |
| campaign B (F2) | 6 / 1 / 3 / 1 | **0** / 4 / 5 / 0 |
| grip mu 0.4 | 1 / 3 / 12 / 37 | **0** / 4 / 12 / 37 |
| grip mu 0.8 | 4 / 1 / 4 / 16 | **0** / 2 / 5 / 16 |

The frozen code's 19 step locks (store_room F2 8, room_r4 F2 3, worker_stops_in_lane F2 3, store_room B5 4 at mu 0.8,
1 at mu 0.4) are gone; the released robots creep on to the bound and hold there (typically a pair 4.5-5 mm inside,
the plan asking 5.3 mm). A1 passes for the rule.

**A2, flips** (campaign A twins): 13 of 280 pairs (frozen 15): store_room F2 9 (11), gate_right_swing F2 1 (1),
door_then_beam F2 3 (3); B5 0 (0). Not significantly lower.

**A3, outcomes** (`compare_variants.py regressions`): reached counts unchanged within noise in every cell (store_room
F2 5 -> 4 of 20, p 0.50; store_room B5 0 -> 0, at mu 0.8 9 -> 10; mu 0.8 F2 18 -> 17); clearance medians unchanged.
One regression by the contact rule: **store_room F2 s18 touches** (rear-left wheel on the door frame, 33 contact cycles,
0.05 mm deep). Frozen, that run locked at the door and never touched; released, it drove on, and from about 40 s later
B5 executed plans whose largest slack grew to 119 mm (the whole 0.12 m base margin) with status `ok` until the wheel
touched: the same pattern as finding (d) (door_105 s20, door_then_beam s5-n).

**Reading.** The rule does what it was designed to do (no lock at the margin's step; creep bounded at 5 mm by the plan
check), but on its own it changes no outcome: the released robots stop at the bound instead, and where they go on,
the base can slide through the whole margin while every plan passes the gate. The gate checks the plan against the
pair's current depth; it does not stop the measured depth from growing cycle after cycle when execution drifts
deeper than the plan predicts. That mechanism (to verify in Task 9) is the one that touches.
