**Where twins first part** (campaign A; chicane: F2 `plan_seed` 20, B5 `end` 20). The histogram of the first
diverging decision: F2 `plan_seed` 130 of 140, `plan_homotopy` 3,
none 7; B5 `status` / `gate_unsafe` 60 (gate trips at a pair outside its margin asking just over 5 mm, e.g. 4.94
against 5.05 mm), `end` 26, none 54. Both histograms mislead:
- **F2's `plan_seed` divergences are mostly ties.** At the first one the two best feasible merits of one side differ by
  a median 1.2e-12 relative (about 80 % below 2e-11; 20 of 130 above 1e-10, the largest 0.048). Most are likely one
  solution listed twice: the planner merges seeds whose control points agree within 0.02, but the log does not carry
  the `merged` flag, so the table cannot tell. The label of the winner is then arbitrary.
- **Most flips are continuous, not a discrete choice.** In 12 of the 15 flipped pairs the plans' paths first part by
  more than 1 mm when the two robots are 0.1-1.3 mm apart (door_then_beam s15: 0.02 mm), and in 11 of the 13 that are
  not feasibility flips the same seed wins on both sides there (store_room s14, s16 differ). Tie labels often differ
  earlier; the first non-tie label difference comes at or after the path's parting. The medium planner amplifies
  sub-millimetre state differences continuously.
- **Two flips are feasibility flips:** gate_right_swing s7 (12.4 s): `fold_back` is infeasible in one twin (merit
  11.29) and feasible in the other (1.82, against `previous` 0.69), and that twin takes the costlier `fold_back`
  (presumably the waiting rule); door_then_beam s18 (15.6 s): both seeds infeasible in one twin, `fold_back` feasible
  in the other. The robot states differ by at most one float32 step (9.5e-7 m) up to there; plans then 0.21 and 0.33 m
  apart, one twin reaches, the other holds. A threshold crossed by a measured value (spec 3.4: enter/leave thresholds
  or a dwell), not a merit band.
- **B5's gate trips never change an outcome** (0 flips): B5 fails store_room and both low beams in every run, plain or
  twin, and passes the rest.

**Refined (CP1, user decision; corrected after the refinement's review).** `divergence.py` now treats two winners as
one solution when their plans agree within 1 mm, or when their merits agree within 1e-9 relative or one is merged; it
adds `plan_path` (the plan's path parting by more than 1 mm) and notes where the sets of feasible seeds first differ
(not a decision: 49 of the 63 pairs whose first difference was there kept the winner and changed nothing). Re-run on
the same logs, first diverging decision, campaign A: F2 `plan_path` 104, `plan_seed` 20, `plan_homotopy` 2,
`plan_status` 1, none 13; B5 unchanged. The 15 flips:

| Kind | Flips | First divergence | Robots apart there |
|---|---|---|---|
| discrete | 2 | gate_right_swing s7: `plan_seed` `previous` against `fold_back` (feasible in one twin only, merit 1.82 against 11.29; `fold_back` wins on progress); door_then_beam s18: `plan_status` infeasible against ok | below 1e-6 m |
| continuous | 13 | `plan_path` 11 (plans 3.5-43 mm apart under the same winner), `plan_seed` 2 (store_room s14, s16, distinct plans) | 0.02-1.5 mm |

Commitment can target the two discrete flips (a seed's or the plan's feasibility at its threshold); the thirteen
continuous ones are the medium planner amplifying sub-millimetre state differences, outside what commitment fixes.
(The first version of this table counted four discrete flips: store_room s5 and s13 were the same plan under two labels,
11 and 0.24 um apart.)

## Gate rule

`slack_gate_inside: absolute` ([configs/b5_absolute.yaml](configs/b5_absolute.yaml)), `e1_ensembles.sh variant ... abs`,
`experiment_runs/e1/abs`, 2026-11-08 20:47-22:34 (1340 runs, none failed), against the frozen set at the same
settings. Reports in `experiment_runs/e1/reports`.

**Holds through the last 10 s** (`compare_variants.py locks`; corrected after the task's review). Classes by the gate's
worst row in the final 500 cycles (constant while the robot is braked): `lock` = a pair 0-5 mm inside its margin, every
failing plan asking at most 5 mm (the frozen rule's step: the absolute rule would pass these); `over` = a pair 0-5 mm
inside, plans asking more than 5 mm (from just over the absolute bound, 5.3 mm, up to 354 mm at the low beams, where
both rules hold alike); `deep` = a pair 5 mm or more inside; `outside` = a pair outside its margin:

| Set | Frozen: lock / over / deep / outside | Absolute: lock / over / deep / outside |
|---|---|---|
| campaign A (plain runs) | 8 / 8 / 21 / 37 | 0 / 10 / 27 / 36 |
| campaign B (F2) | 6 / 1 / 3 / 1 | 0 / 4 / 5 / 0 |
| grip mu 0.4 | 1 / 3 / 12 / 37 | 0 / 4 / 12 / 37 |
| grip mu 0.8 | 4 / 1 / 4 / 16 | 0 / 2 / 5 / 16 |

Under the absolute rule a failing plan always asks more than 5 mm, so `lock` is zero by construction; the evidence is
what became of the frozen code's 19 step locks (store_room F2 8, room_r4 F2 3, worker_stops_in_lane F2 3, store_room F2
s11 at mu 0.4, store_room B5 4 at mu 0.8): 2 reach the goal (store_room B5 mu 0.8 s5, s20), 7 are released but end
without reaching it (one of them, store_room F2 s18, touches), 7 hold `over` (a pair 4.5-5 mm inside, plans asking
5.3-5.6 mm: the robot crept to the bound) and 3 end `deep` (store_room F2 s9 at 6.5 mm, s17 at 20.2 mm, store_room B5
mu 0.8 s8 at 5.95 mm). By the spec's wording of A1 (a final hold with 0 < violation < 5 mm) the absolute rule still has
20 such holds: whether A1 counts only the step's locks is the user's ruling (CP2). New `deep` holds under the absolute
rule, for E2 (cause to attribute there): store_room B5 s1; store_room F2 s3, s9, s13, s15, s17; room_r4 F2 s9, s17;
store_room B5 mu 0.8 s8.

**First-trip holds** (store_room: a pair outside its margin, plans asking 5-6 mm): frozen B5 in 17 of 20 runs (20
episodes), F2 in 4 (4 episodes); absolute B5 17 (20), F2 3 (3); each 0.02-0.08 s. No flip site (spec 3.1: only holds of
seconds would be).

**A2, flips** (twins): campaign A 13 of 280 pairs (frozen 15): store_room F2 9 (11), gate_right_swing F2 1 (1),
door_then_beam F2 3 (3), B5 0 (0); chicane 0 of 40 (0). Not significantly lower.

**A3, outcomes** (`compare_variants.py regressions`): reached counts within noise in every cell (store_room F2 5 -> 4
of 20, p 0.50; store_room B5 0 -> 0; at mu 0.8 B5 9 -> 10, p 0.74, F2 18 -> 17, p 0.50); clearance medians within 1 mm.
One regression by the contact rule: **store_room F2 s18 touches** (rear-left wheel on door S's frame, 33 contact
cycles, 0.13 mm deep, true minimum clearance -0.15 mm). Frozen, it locked at the door from 48.7 s and never touched.
Released at about 49 s, it went on; from 63 s to the first contact at 79.4 s every plan passed the gate (status `ok`)
while the largest slack of the gate's rows grew from 5 to 119.8 mm. A passing slack s means some pair is already at
least s - 0.1 mm inside its margin, so the base went through its whole 0.12 m margin: here during an in-place turn
(yaw -1.96 to -2.50 rad, 5 cm of translation) under a three-cycle command chatter, the depth growing about 0.55 mm per
three cycles against the 0.1 mm allowance. door_105 s20 and door_then_beam s5-n show the same rise to about 120 mm.

**Reading.** The rule removes the step lock as designed, but on its own changes no outcome: most released robots stop
at the bound or end elsewhere without reaching. Its one new contact shows the gate's weakness inside a margin: the
gate compares each plan with the pair's current depth, and nothing stops that depth from growing cycle after cycle
when the robot ends each cycle deeper than the plan predicted. The candidates for that gap, to test in Task 9: the
plant against B5's model (the skid turn's lag and chatter) and the linearization error of the collision row.

## The measured-depth guard and the infeasibility fallback (CP3 variants)

Built at CP3 (user decision): the **measured-depth guard** (`slack_gate_depth_guard_m`: hold when a pair of the
cycle's rows is measured deeper than its reference, T or its depth when first seen deeper, by more than the
tolerance; a missing pair keeps its reference 1 s) and the **infeasibility fallback** (`medium_max_infeasible_s`: B5
leaves a run of infeasible medium plans for nav2 after that long; stale_reused plans neither start nor end a run).
Both off by default. Ensembles 2026-11-09 00:37-11:32 (the host slept 00:49-08:29; 11 F2 runs that spanned the sleep
were deleted and rerun): **combined** = absolute + guard 2 mm + fallback 2 s
([configs/b5_e1_combined.yaml](configs/b5_e1_combined.yaml), `experiment_runs/e1/e1c`, 1340 runs) and **fallback
alone** ([configs/b5_infeasible.yaml](configs/b5_infeasible.yaml), `experiment_runs/e1/inf`, F2 only, 860 runs).

Plain runs (twins left out):

| Variant | Reached | Runs with contacts |
|---|---|---|
| frozen | 828 / 1020 | 2 (twins: 2 more) |
| absolute | 827 / 1020 | 3 |
| combined | 806 / 1020 | 0 (twins: 0) |
| fallback alone (F2) | 636 / 700 (frozen F2 639) | 0 (twins: 0) |

- **Combined:** every contact gone, at the price of 22 reached runs. Of the 21 runs that reached frozen but not
  combined, 18 end in the guard's permanent hold 7-23 mm inside the base margin (about 100 mm from the frame): a trip
  never raises the reference, braking ends past it, so every later cycle trips. Significant in one cell: store_room
  B5 at mu 0.8, 9 -> 0 of 20 (p 0.001): frozen, those runs passed by creeping about 10 mm into the margin. The guard's
  2 mm above the 5 mm bound is far tighter than the 120 mm the contacts used.
- **Fallback alone:** every F2 contact gone without the holds: per cell reached within one run of frozen (losses in
  door_105 x2, door_105_mission, gate_right_swing, room_r4; gains in worker_stops_in_lane, store_room mu 0.8; none
  significant); the fallback fired in 56 runs. The contacts all came while B5 followed infeasible medium plans; leaving
  them for the Nav2 route avoids the drift at its source.
- **Flips** (campaign A twins): frozen 15 of 280, absolute 13, combined 14; fallback alone 16 of 140 F2 pairs (frozen
  F2 15 of 140). No variant changes them.

## Findings (c) and (d): the depth ratchet

Instrument (3880257): with `plant_campaign --diagnostics` the State frames carry `diag` = [the deepest pair among the
gated rows with slack (its current violation), that pair's stage-1 slack (the plan's linearized violation 0.1 s
ahead), the plan's largest linearization error over its rows of stages 1-10]; off by default (logs unchanged). Each
contact run rerun with it reproduces its original's State and Event frames exactly (`experiment_runs/e1/ratchet`; the
baseline originals' Plans frames predate the seeds' `merged` entry). The trace does not name the pair. The base state
reaches B5 without delay in the realistic profile, so the depths are not biased by a delayed state.

**The door cells** (door_105_mission F2 s20, frozen and absolute alike: finding d; door_then_beam F2 s5-n), from the
first cycle a gated pair is more than 5 mm inside its margin to the contact:

| Run | Depth (mm) | Measured gain per 0.1 s (p10 / median / p90, mm) | Plan's stage-1 gain (median, mm) | Linearization error (median / p90 / max, mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| door_105 s20 | 5.2 -> 119.6 in 6.8 s | 1.21 / 1.69 / 2.04 | -0.91 (every plan: out, at most -0.31) | 0.16 / 1.7 / 3.7 |
| door_then_beam s5-n | 5.1 -> 120.0 in 10 s | 0.81 / 1.14 / 1.49 | -0.87 (out) | 0.13 / 1.5 / 8.0 |

Every plan predicts the deepest pair leaving and every 0.1 s the robot ends 1-2 mm deeper (0.02-0.8 mm per 20 ms
cycle, rising steadily, so most likely the same pair). Meanwhile the base is almost at rest: forward speed median 0.002
m/s, an in-place turn of about 0.03 rad/s, executed speed about 0.004 m/s. Two candidates fit: B5's model of the base
against the plant's wheel-contact base (sideslip, turn response; E2), or commands below the breakaway that the plant
does not execute as B5 assumes (stiction; as in the RPP rotate deadlock). They are told apart by rolling B5's model
out with the executed command against the measured state, and by projecting the measured pose change onto the pair's
distance gradient per degree of freedom (not done). The ratchet starts right after the last feasible medium plan
(34.0 -> 34.66 s; 16.0 -> 18.98 s): the infeasible references keep B5 there; they do not explain the prediction gap.

**store_room F2 abs s18** is different: depth above 5 mm for about 20 s of the window, unbroken from 62.8 s to the
contact at 79.4 s; the median measured and predicted gains are both 0 and 54 % of its plans predict a deeper pair (up
to 5.6 mm, within the absolute rule's allowance below the bound); the linearization error spikes (max 79 mm) during
an in-place turn under command chatter. Drift and linearization error both take part; not attributed further.

**door_then_beam F2 s15-n** (the panel on the low beam): the plans see no conflict (stage-1 slack 0) while the gated
depth rises from 0 to 13 mm in 0.6 s; the plan's largest linearization error (over all rows, not necessarily this
pair) is 10-39 mm already from 48 s and 31-95 mm in the last `ok` cycles; the gate holds from 50.76 s, and the depth
then rises 14 -> 66 mm in 0.14 s while braking (beyond the panel's 0.05 m margin: B5's model sees 16 mm of
penetration where the plant touches by 1 mm, or the deepest pair switched to a base row). Linearization error of the
collision rows is the candidate (E4); not attributed to a pair.

In all of these the measured depth grows for many cycles past 5 mm before anything touches; a guard on the measured
depth would see it, with the caveat that the trace's depth covers only pairs with slack (CP3).

**Following infeasible medium plans.** In door_105 s20 the medium plans are infeasible throughout the last 7 s before
the contact, in door_then_beam s5-n 12.5 s, s15-n 27 s; B5 follows them all the way (the medium switch falls back to
nav2 only for no plan, a stale plan, another route or an offset beyond 0.3 m, not for infeasibility).

**Finding (c), gate_right_swing F2 left starts.** Twin pairs on every seed (frozen): the first side is the same in
both twins of 19 of 20 pairs (s19: left against right, both fail); which side F2 takes follows the seed's start
perturbation (1 mm, 1 mrad). Left starts fail 3 of 5 runs, right starts 1 of 33 (p 0.0045, but twins are not
independent: the 5 left starts are 3 seeds; plain runs only, 2 of 3 against 0 of 16, p 0.018; 2 runs never swing). Not
a rounding flip, so no commitment fix; the left swing at this gate fails on 2 of 3 seeds. Why (the plan or
its execution at the posts) is open; handed on with this evidence (E2 first: the arm and base tracking through the
gate).
