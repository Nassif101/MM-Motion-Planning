## Predictability: the first fit

margin = constant + weights x (v2, w2, qd2) of the plan's window, fitted on 70 % of the runs that reached and judged on the other 30 % (split by run): the held-out mean margin against the constant bound with the same held-out coverage. Go: a saving of at least 30 % at a coverage within 3 points of 95 %. The offset is set on the same held-out rows its coverage is measured on: the coverage is an in-sample calibration check, not a test of future coverage, and it is marginal over the pooled rows, not per run or per regime. The fit minimises a least-squares surrogate over the bins, not the mean margin, so its saving is not the best its form can do.

Saving, binned: the same with each feature bin's own quantile in place of the linear form (a diagnostic comparison, not an upper bound on what the features predict, and not the criterion).
