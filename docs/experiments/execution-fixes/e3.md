What the tool reports, and what to check on the ensembles before reading anything into it:

- `e_track` is measured from B5's state, so it holds the arm's offset between its streamed reference and its measured
  joints at the planning cycle; `e_state` is that offset alone. In this run the two are of the same size at 0.1 s
  while the arm moves (about 9 to 10 mm at the 95th percentile on the arm rows): the short-lookahead error may be
  mostly the standing offset, not an error that builds up over the lookahead.
- `e_lin` at the traced stages is at most 0.2 mm at the 95th percentile: the solved plan is a small step from its
  nominal. This is the error at the stages; the error between stages is E4's and is not in the trace.
- `e_total` grows with the lookahead much faster than `e_track` (panel, arm regime: 12.9 mm at 0.1 s, 49.1 mm at
  1.0 s): it also holds B5 changing its plan at later cycles, which is not a tracking error.
- The run has no arm or panel row in the `straight` regime: under this beam the arm moves throughout. The tightness
  cells' straight approaches (Task 3) are what supplies that regime.

What the dry runs show (5 seeds per value: indications, not results):

- **The gap's reach count drops between 0.26 and 0.22 m.** From 0.42 to 0.30 m the clearance is the same 43 to 45 mm:
  F2 threads the vertical panel at the margin whatever the width. This is not a safe-pass boundary: at 0.26 m one of
  the four runs that reached (seed 2) had a minimum clearance of 18.4 mm, below the spec's 20 mm safety floor. The
  steps 0.30, 0.27, 0.24, 0.21 m are an exploratory grid around the drop. A margin of 0.02 m in place of 0.05 m
  allows 0.03 m more per side in the constraint; whether the planner's transition moves by that much is not measured.
- **The beam shows no ordered transition.** Passing is 5, 3, 5, 4 and 3 of 5 from 1.23 to 1.15 m, and every failure
  ends in a hold or a timeout, none in a contact. That names how the runs end, not what limits them: tracking,
  feasibility, planning or the gate's policy are not told apart here.
- **The grid is exploratory (Task 3 review I2).** Only the 0.05 m margin was piloted. The levels are not shown to
  bracket where each swept margin stops passing safely. In the sweep a cell where every margin passes, every margin
  fails, or the outcome is not ordered in the margin is reported as uninformative and takes no part in a threshold.

- **The slow variant is not a usable control.** A slower reference does not give a slow passage: the gap that passes
  5 of 5 at 0.24 m/s passes 1 of 5 at 0.18 m/s and 0 of 5 at 0.12 m/s. The rows it adds to the `straight` regime are
  again long near-standstills before the obstacle (up to 13,800 rows in one run), not passages.
- **Slower was not better tracked where it did pass.** Per run, the 95th-percentile tracking error at 0.5 s on the
  arm and panel rows against the beam: 0.8 to 4.6 mm at 0.24 m/s, 3.6 to 19.1 mm at 0.18 m/s (5 runs each).
- Why a slower reference stops the gap's passage is not known (the medium planner's progress at low speed, or the
  base near its breakaway, are candidates; neither was examined). It bears on E3's premise that tracking is good, and
  passage easy, when slow.
- The 0.5 s per-run figures on the step 1 runs, for orientation only (5 runs per cell): beam straight 0.8 to 4.6 mm,
  beam turn 1.8 to 12.3 mm, gap straight 8.3 to 10.9 mm, gap turn 3.0 to 12.8 mm.

The contact and below-20 mm columns count every run, reached or not. What the tables say:

- **A smaller margin does not open these cells.** The beam passes about 9 in 10 at every margin. The realistic gap
  passes 86, 106, 106 and 97 of 160: more at 0.04 m than at 0.05 m, no more below it.
- **The runs that do not reach stall in the passage.** Of the 666 runs that did not reach, 482 stalled (45 s without
  progress) and 184 timed out, almost all inside the passage on the straight cells (the turn cells were not
  located). In them the medium planner is infeasible in about 70 % of its cycles (median) and capped in 42 to 80 %,
  against about 1 % and 20 to 29 % in the runs that reach. Why the medium planner fails there was not examined.
- **Clearance follows the margin.** On the gap, the median clearance of the runs that reach falls by 10 mm per
  0.01 m of margin in both modes (ideal 46.5, 36.4, 26.1, 16.0 mm).
- **Tracking costs a few millimetres.** The realistic median is 2 to 3 mm below the ideal one on the gap and 2 to
  9 mm on the beam; the 5th percentile 4 to 10 mm below.
- **The realistic plant does not pass fewer.** No cell and margin has a one-sided Fisher p below 0.05 for the
  realistic plant passing fewer (the smallest: 0.064, `e3_gap_turn_2` at 0.02 m). On the gap it passes more (395 of
  640 against 321). The two modes also differ in B5's model (the identified one against the unicycle), so this is not
  tracking alone. The smallest safe margin is one step larger on the realistic plant on 9 of the 14 cells that have
  one in both modes (0.04 against 0.03 m on six gap cells, 0.05 against 0.04 m on three beam turn cells).
- **No fixed margin is safe on every cell** by the definition (zero contacts and never below 20 mm in every run). At
  0.05 m, four realistic gap cells fail it through runs that did not reach: they stayed in the passage on plans with
  slack (for example `e3_gap_turn_3` seed 16: 10.0 mm, 7 executed slack plans). The ideal plant has such runs too
  (`e3_gap_straight_4` at 0.05 m, seed 19: a contact, -23.9 mm, 31 executed slack plans), so they are not a tracking
  effect. Among the runs that reach, 0.05 m keeps 28 mm or more everywhere and 0.04 m does not (-0.2 mm in one gap
  run, 74 executed slack plans).
- **Informative cells** by the rule fixed before the sweep: 9 of 16 realistic, 10 of 16 ideal. With pass rates that
  do not move with the margin, "informative" marks noise that happens to be ordered at least as often as a threshold.

## Problems and resolutions

| Problem | Cause | Resolution | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| The trace of a prepared (RTI) cycle reported a mover's update as linearization error (60 mm in the review's case) | The exact distance was taken against the feedback step's world, the rows against the preparation's | A prepared cycle is traced against the preparation's world and time | Task 1 review C1 |
| A trace-only log could not be analysed | The sent command and the tracking estimate are diagnostics keys | The row trace turns the diagnostics on | Task 1 review I1 |
| A goal-reached step shifted the trace's phase | The trace counted `step()` calls | Once per 0.1 s of the clock, on the first solved cycle | Task 1 review I2 |
| A zeroed gradient passed every trace test | The tests checked shapes and a 0.05 m bound | An oracle test against the collision model (distance 1e-9, gradient 0.05) | Task 1 review |
| The analysis could score a run with a tracking model it did not plan with (21 mm of false error on a unicycle run in the review's case) | The prior was the installed model file whatever the log said | Each run is resolved to its own model from the hello's hash; another file is refused unless asked for as a counterfactual; the report names the models | Task 2 review C1 |
| A 0.05 s stage would take a row's realised state at 0.4 s and label it 0.5 s | The stage's cycles were rounded | A stage that is not a whole number of cycles is refused; the elapsed time is checked | Task 2 review C2 |
| The history table could not show memory | It correlated the error with the previous plan's window, mostly future motion | History is the commands sent in the 0.5 s before the planning epoch, labelled exploratory; the ruling uses a held-out comparison at CP1 (plan Task 5 amendment) | Task 2 review I1 |
| The beam after the turn held once at its easiest step (1.23 m, 4 of 5) | A step 1 must pass 5 of 5 with the 0.05 m margin | The beam's steps moved up 0.02 m (1.25 to 1.19 m); both beam cells 5 of 5 | Task 3 |
| The turn cells' rows against the obstacle were not in a turning regime (1 to 5 %) | The obstacle 1.5 m after the turn: the turn ends before the rows are active (0.8 m) | The obstacle 0.5 m after the turn's end (48 % and 14 %) | Task 3 |
| A synthetic cell could only drive a straight line | `make_case` built the route from the start and the goal | A scenario's own `route`, checked to run from the start to the goal | Task 3 |
| Four ideal runs spanned a host sleep (lid closed at 17:54, then a thermal emergency sleep; 97 s) | `caffeinate -i` does not stop a lid-close sleep; the medium planner's cap is wall-clock time | The four runs moved to `sweep_slept`, kept, and run again; the power log checked for both legs | Task 4 |
| The trace report of a 320-run leg takes about 15 minutes and 1 GB | All rows of all runs are held in memory | Accepted for Task 4; the per-run statistics of CP1 can go run by run | Task 4 |
