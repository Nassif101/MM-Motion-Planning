What the tool reports, and what to check on the ensembles before reading anything into it:

- `e_track` is measured from B5's state, so it holds the arm's offset between its streamed reference and its measured
  joints at the planning cycle; `e_state` is that offset alone. In this run the two are of the same size at 0.1 s
  while the arm moves (about 9 to 10 mm at the 95th percentile on the arm rows): the short-lookahead error may be
  mostly the standing offset, not an error that builds up over the lookahead.
- `e_lin` at the traced stages is at most 0.2 mm at the 95th percentile: the solved plan is a small step from its
  nominal. This is the error at the stages; the error between stages is E4's and is not in the trace.
- `e_total` grows with the lookahead much faster than `e_track` (panel, arm regime: 12.9 mm at 0.1 s, 49.1 mm at
  1.0 s): it also holds B5 changing its plan at later cycles, which is not a tracking error.
- The run has no arm or panel row in the `straight` regime: under this beam the arm moves throughout. The tightness
  cells' straight approaches (Task 3) are what supplies that regime.

[corpus_e3_tight.json](corpus_e3_tight.json). All in the open delivery yard, starting in carry, F2.

- **Beam:** the low-beam cells' beam (0.2 m deep, 3.0 m across, 0.2 m thick), its underside at 1.25, 1.23, 1.21 and
  1.19 m.
- **Gap:** two braces raised above the base (0.4 m deep, 0.75 to 2.2 m high), their faces 0.30, 0.27, 0.24 and 0.21 m

What the dry runs show (5 seeds per value: indications, not results):

- **The gap has a threshold.** Passing ends between 0.26 and 0.22 m. From 0.42 to 0.30 m the clearance is the same
  43 to 45 mm: F2 threads the vertical panel at the margin whatever the width, so those widths do not differ in
  tightness. The steps span the threshold: 0.30 (passes), 0.27, 0.24, 0.21 m. Lowering the margin from 0.05 to 0.02 m
  frees 0.03 m per side, the steps' spacing.
- **The beam does not.** Passing falls from 5 of 5 at 1.23 m to 3 of 5 at 1.15 m, not in order (1.19 m: 5 of 5), and
  every failure is a hold or a timeout, none a contact. Over this range the beam's passage is limited by B5 holding,
  not by a height the panel cannot get under.

## Problems and resolutions

| Problem | Cause | Resolution | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| The trace of a prepared (RTI) cycle reported a mover's update as linearization error (60 mm in the review's case) | The exact distance was taken against the feedback step's world, the rows against the preparation's | A prepared cycle is traced against the preparation's world and time | Task 1 review C1 |
| A trace-only log could not be analysed | The sent command and the tracking estimate are diagnostics keys | The row trace turns the diagnostics on | Task 1 review I1 |
| A goal-reached step shifted the trace's phase | The trace counted `step()` calls | Once per 0.1 s of the clock, on the first solved cycle | Task 1 review I2 |
| A zeroed gradient passed every trace test | The tests checked shapes and a 0.05 m bound | An oracle test against the collision model (distance 1e-9, gradient 0.05) | Task 1 review |
| The analysis could score a run with a tracking model it did not plan with (21 mm of false error on a unicycle run in the review's case) | The prior was the installed model file whatever the log said | Each run is resolved to its own model from the hello's hash; another file is refused unless asked for as a counterfactual; the report names the models | Task 2 review C1 |
| A 0.05 s stage would take a row's realised state at 0.4 s and label it 0.5 s | The stage's cycles were rounded | A stage that is not a whole number of cycles is refused; the elapsed time is checked | Task 2 review C2 |
| The history table could not show memory | It correlated the error with the previous plan's window, mostly future motion | History is the commands sent in the 0.5 s before the planning epoch, labelled exploratory; the ruling uses a held-out comparison at CP1 (plan Task 5 amendment) | Task 2 review I1 |
| The beam after the turn held once at its easiest step (1.23 m, 4 of 5) | A step 1 must pass 5 of 5 with the 0.05 m margin | The beam's steps moved up 0.02 m (1.25 to 1.19 m); both beam cells 5 of 5 | Task 3 |
| The turn cells' rows against the obstacle were not in a turning regime (1 to 5 %) | The obstacle 1.5 m after the turn: the turn ends before the rows are active (0.8 m) | The obstacle 0.5 m after the turn's end (48 % and 14 %) | Task 3 |
| A synthetic cell could only drive a straight line | `make_case` built the route from the start and the goal | A scenario's own `route`, checked to run from the start to the goal | Task 3 |
