## Measurement (stage 1, Task 4; 2026-11-12)

### The runs and their reproducibility

18 closed-loop runs, recorded with `--diagnostics` (the deepest gated pair, its gradient and kink, the command B5
sent, and the first control of B5's plan before the output stage):

- E1's five depth-ratchet runs;
- store_room for B5 alone, seeds 1-5;
- gate_right_swing for F2, the failing seeds 4, 7, 11 and 19 and the passing seeds 1, 2, 3 and 5.

Each run used its baseline's B5 config. Each reproduces its original, from `experiment_runs/e1/{ratchet,abs,absinf}`,
frame by frame: State and Event frames are identical apart from the diagnostics keys (`model_rollout.py same-run`,
18 of 18, both before and after `diag_plan_u0` was added). The diagnostics therefore change nothing, and the
measurement describes the very runs E1 analysed.

Beside them:

- an open-loop excitation through `plant_replay`, with no B5: 31 segments of steps, ramps and turns, carry pose;
- the arm feedforward check (T8).

The excitation's breakaway steps go below and above the plant's breakaway, which is 0.005 m/s and 0.02 rad/s.

### Finding 1: the depth ratchet is B5's output stage, not the plant

In every cycle of the three ratchet episodes (E1's window: from the deepest pair 5 mm inside its margin to the
contact), the command B5 sent turns the base the other way from the first control of B5's own plan:

| Run | Cycles | Plan's yaw rate (median) | Sent yaw rate (median) | Depth change per 0.1 s: plan's control | Sent command | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| door_105_mission F2 s20 | 341 | -0.026 rad/s | +0.054 rad/s | -0.88 mm | +2.05 mm | +1.68 mm |
| door_then_beam F2 s5-n | 501 | +0.026 | -0.054 | -0.86 | +1.91 | +1.15 |
| store_room F2 abs s18 | 831 | +0.026 | -0.054 | -0.72 | +1.49 | +0.69 |

The depth changes in the table are one-cycle rollouts of B5's own model from B5's state, projected on the pair's
distance gradient at that state, summed and scaled to 0.1 s. "Measured" is the B5 state's actual change, projected
the same way. Its sum matches the logged depth change: 114.3 mm against 114.1 mm over door_105's episode. All
windows are smooth (no kink). The base's commanded forward speed is 0 throughout: the base turns in place.

The plan's control predicts the pair leaving (-0.7 to -0.9 mm per 0.1 s, as E1 found from the plans' stage-1 slack).
B5's own model, fed the sent command, predicts it going in faster than it does (+1.5 to +2.1 mm). The plant then
executes less of that turn than the model assumes (Finding 2), which slows the drift but does not cause it.

**The mechanism** (`controller.cpp`, the command path of an `ok` cycle):

1. The plan's first control is clamped to the rate band around the command sent last: +-0.016 rad/s per 20 ms
   cycle, from `yaw_accel_radps2` 0.8.
2. `breakaway_output` then raises a command below the breakaway to the breakaway, 0.07 rad/s (the envelope's
   `breakaway_yaw_radps`, Unity's value), in the clamped command's sign, whenever the base reads "at rest" (measured
   |omega| below `rest_yaw_radps`, 0.02).

Once one kick has gone out at +0.07, a plan asking for -0.026 is clamped to +0.054. If the base reads at rest, the
command is boosted back to +0.07, the old sign. The sent yaw rates take exactly three levels in door_105's episode:
0.070 (127 cycles), 0.054 (127) and 0.038 (87), a kick and its rate-limited decay. The plant's measured yaw rate
alternates between about 0.026 and 0.002 rad/s, so the base reads "at rest" every few cycles and the kick repeats.
The command can never cross zero.

The status is `ok` and the gate passes in every cycle (absolute rule; the plans themselves are safe). This is B5
logic. It does not depend on this plant except through how often the base reads at rest. Whether Unity shows it is
not measured (E5).

E1's two candidates were (a) B5's base model against the wheel-contact base and (b) sub-breakaway commands the plant
does not execute as B5 assumes. The data supports (b) in a sharper form: B5's own output stage turns a small planned
correction into a kick the other way.

### Finding 2: the plant's base against B5's model

The one-stage (0.1 s) error of B5's model with the command B5 sent, over every 0.1 s window of the 18 runs. The
"twist" rows feed the measured twist instead of the command:

| Horizon | Input | forward p50/p90/max (mm) | lateral p50/p90/max (mm) | yaw p50/p90/max (mrad) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 s | command | 0.08 / 0.63 / 19.4 | 0.00 / 0.49 / 10.5 | 0.00 / 2.64 / 20.3 |
| 0.1 s | measured twist | 0.00 / 0.19 / 2.2 | 0.00 / 0.51 / 10.3 | 0.00 / 0.37 / 4.6 |

The yaw and forward errors are command tracking: the measured twist removes 86 % of the yaw p90. The lateral error is
kinematic: no twist input removes it, because the unicycle has no lateral motion.

**Turn response.** The yaw rate the base achieves, as a share of the command:

| abs omega (rad/s) | below 0.02 | 0.02-0.07 | 0.07-0.2 | 0.2-0.6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| open loop, in place | 0.04 | 0.50 | 0.72 | 0.90 |
| closed loop, 0.1-0.3 m/s | 0.10 | 0.51 | 0.85 | 0.95 |

The open-loop ratios are from the excitation, which turns in place. The closed-loop ratios are from the 18 runs at
0.1-0.3 m/s. The understeer grows sharply as the yaw rate falls towards the plant's 0.02 rad/s breakaway. The ratchet
episodes command 0.038-0.07 rad/s, where the base achieves about half.

**Delays and lags** (alignment fits, delays to +-0.01 s; closed-loop unless stated):

| Channel | Delay | Time constant | Gain | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v | 0.000 s | 0.030 s | 0.99 | open loop 0.035 / 0.235 s: the plant's acceleration ramp on steps, which B5's own limits avoid |
| omega | 0.010 s | 0.000 s | 0.87 | the gain is the turn response above, nonlinear in omega |
| arm, pipeline (command to joint) | 0.005-0.045 s | 0-0.04 s | 0.98-1.01 | delay + time constant 0.035-0.045 s on every joint |
| arm, servo (reference to joint) | 0.030-0.035 s | 0 | 0.99-1.00 | the plant's 30 ms arm command delay |

The arm's servo fit leaves out 18243 intervals as `equal_states`. These are not reanchors. 97 % of those records have
a reference velocity of exactly 0, and none has a requested joint velocity above 1e-3 rad/s: they are arm holds with
no trajectory (`ArmCommandPath::b5_state` returns the measured joints then). The 818 `at_limit` intervals are
reference velocities at the 0.6 rad/s limit. The servo fit keeps 53466 intervals.

**Sideslip.** Against the executed twist (the lateral dynamics alone), the lateral speed follows v x omega with no
build-up, in both data sets. The coefficients disagree fourfold:

| Data | Transitions | Coefficient (lateral m/s per v x omega) | Time constant | Delay | rms (m/s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| open-loop excitation (in-place and 0.15-0.3 m/s turns, carry) | 12 | 0.049 | 0.000 s | 0.020 s | 0.0067 |
| the 18 closed-loop runs | 167 | 0.200 | 0.000 s | 0.005 s | 0.0044 |

Against the command, the open-loop delay is 0.155 s, which is the base's own lag. The build-up is below 0.1 s in both,
so there is no case for a lateral state.

The closed-loop coefficient is the one B5's commands meet. The difference is not explained here: arm poses and loads,
turn histories, and chatter in the finite-difference lateral speed are all candidates. Task 6's identification fits it
per pose and on both data sets.

**Shares of the one-stage error per short-list term.** A Python unicycle, cross-checked against B5's model, over all
14498 non-overlapping 0.1 s windows. Each term is added to the sent command; RMS and p90 of the affected axis:

| Term | Axis | Today (rms / p90) | With the term | Share of the error removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| turn gain (the closed-loop ratios above, per abs omega) | yaw | 1.65 / 2.63 mrad | 1.02 / 1.24 mrad | 38 % / 53 % |
| sideslip 0.049 v omega (open-loop coefficient) | lateral | 0.51 / 0.49 mm | 0.46 / 0.42 mm | 9 % / 14 % |
| the same, turning windows only (abs v omega > 0.02, 1939 windows) | lateral | 1.23 / 1.65 mm | 1.09 / 1.40 mm | 11 % / 15 % |
| sideslip 0.20 v omega (closed-loop coefficient), turning windows | lateral | 1.23 / 1.65 mm | 0.95 / 1.10 mm | 23 % / 33 % |
| the same, all windows | lateral | 0.51 / 0.49 mm | 0.42 / 0.53 mm | 18 % / -7 % |
| arm command delay 0.04 s | arm (worst joint) | 1.46 / 1.08 mrad | 1.23 / 0.53 mrad | 15 % / 51 % |
| speed ratio (0.99) | forward | 0.97 / 0.64 mm | unchanged | 0 % |
| base delay compensation (0-0.01 s) | all | | | not resolvable at 20 ms |

On the turning windows, a forward error of 1.97 mm RMS remains that no listed term touches. The twist bins show the
base 6 % slow in turns (speed ratio 0.94 at abs omega 0.2-0.6). That is a candidate outside the short list.

### Finding 3: the arm feedforward (T8)

`motion_ff` on against off, realistic profile with its 30 ms command delay. The commissioning move from carry:

| Move | Base | Max error on / off (mrad, worst joint) | RMS on / off (mrad, worst joint) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 s | still | 18.4 / 18.6 | 5.7 / 5.8 |
| 1.5 s | driving 0.3 m/s, 0.3 rad/s | 18.7 / 18.6 | 5.7 / 5.8 |
| 0.15 s | still | 715 / 717 | 165.6 / 165.8 |
| 0.15 s | driving | 713 / 715 | 164.7 / 164.1 |

The feedforward changes the tracking error by at most 2 % (about 0.1-2 mrad). The arm's error is the 30 ms command
delay, and on the 0.15 s move the torque saturation. Roadmap part (a) is built and on, and its effect is measured:
negligible against the delay.

### Where the latch occurs, and the other hand-off cells

Latched cycles are cycles where the sent yaw rate is at a kick level (|omega| at least 0.038 rad/s) and has the
opposite sign to the plan's. Boosted cycles are cycles where the sent |omega| exceeds the plan's by more than 0.01
rad/s. Deep cycles have the deepest gated pair at least 5 mm inside its margin. Per run:

| Run | Cycles | Latched | Boosted | Deep | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| door_105_mission F2 s20 (frozen and abs alike) | 6949 | 810 | 1263 | 858 | reached, 407 contact cycles |
| door_then_beam F2 s5-n | 3166 | 1044 | 1175 | 1100 | reached, 472 contact cycles |
| store_room F2 abs s18 | 6482 | 920 | 1362 | 1194 | `ok` at the end, 33 contact cycles |
| door_then_beam F2 s15-n (the low beam) | 3133 | 0 | 83 | 225 | reached, 175 contact cycles |
| gate_right_swing F2 s19 (fails) | 5513 | 508 | 1118 | 3576 | holding |
| gate_right_swing F2 s11 (fails) | 3304 | 97 | 141 | 2362 | holding |
| gate_right_swing F2 s4 (fails) | 5820 | 87 | 118 | 74 | `ok`, stalled |
| gate_right_swing F2 s7 (fails) | 3610 | 0 | 122 | 2307 | holding |
| gate_right_swing F2 s1, s2, s3, s5 (reach) | about 1480 each | 0 | 1-5 | 0 | reached |
| store_room B5 s1-s5 | about 4300 each | 0 | 0-4 | about 2300 | holding |

- **The depth ratchet:** the three contact runs whose pair drifts in at rest are the latched ones. door_then_beam
  s15-n, the panel on the low beam, has no latch. It is E1's linearization case, handed to E4.
- **gate_right_swing, finding (c):** three of the four failing seeds latch, in 87 to 508 cycles; s7 does not, though
  it is boosted in 122 cycles. The passing seeds never go 5 mm deep and never latch. The latch is therefore a
  candidate for the failing swings, not yet their shown cause.
- **store_room for B5 alone:** no latch and no boost. The base turns into the 1.05 m door at full speed (0.3 m/s,
  0.2-0.4 rad/s), with the plans seeing no violation. A pair the trace had not carried before (5|3, the door S frame)
  then appears 5-7 mm deep within about 0.2 s. The gate holds, and the braking carries the pair to 12-27 mm (seeds 1
  and 3). Whether that jump is the turn response (understeer, sideslip) or the pair entering the collision rows late
  (the row budget, linearization) is not told apart by this measurement.

## Problems and resolutions

| Problem | Cause | Resolution | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| The State frame's executed block is the measured twist, not the command | The session logs the plant's response | `diag_cmd` (and since Task 4 `diag_plan_u0`) under `--diagnostics` | Task 1 |
| The servo fit excluded a quarter of the intervals as equal states | Arm holds: no trajectory, B5's arm state is the measured one | Recorded as holds, not reanchors; no producer flag needed (Task 3 review ruling) | Task 4 |
| The sideslip build-up was "not identifiable" on closed-loop runs | The transition count took single 20 ms jumps; closed-loop commands ramp | Transitions are changes within 0.5 s, counted once | Task 4 |
| The gradient projection's pooled medians were all 0 | Long held stretches dominate the pool | Per-episode sums on the ratchet windows (Finding 1) | Task 4 |
| The depth ratchet's cause (E1's open question) | B5's breakaway boost in the old command's sign, with the rate band, latches the turn against the plan | To CP1 | Task 4, Finding 1 |
