# Phase 5A: executor rulings and deferred minors

Exported from the plan's execution ledger at the end of Phase 5A (2026-11-02), in the order they were made. A ruling is a
decision the executor took where the plan or spec left it open or was wrong, with its cost if wrong; a deferred minor is
a review finding left for later. User decisions are recorded in [phase5a.md](../phase5a.md) and the roadmap's decision
log; the problems and their resolutions in phase5a.md's table.
